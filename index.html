GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I'm a passionate Web Developer

I am a website developer with a strong interest in creating digital experiences that are modern, functional and easy to use. I enjoy taking an idea and turning it into a complete website that people can interact with and enjoy.

I recently graduated from the FNB App Academy, where I developed my knowledge and practical skills in software and web development. The experience introduced me to different aspects of development and strengthened my interest in building real-world digital projects.

One of the things I enjoy most about web development is the process of creating something from the ground up. I enjoy working with HTML, CSS and JavaScript to create websites, develop layouts, design different sections and add functionality that makes websites interactive and useful for users.

I am also competent in using AI tools to assist with website development. I use AI as a development tool to help with ideas, problem-solving, learning, improving code and creating websites more efficiently, while continuing to develop my own technical skills and understanding.

I am also someone who enjoys learning. Technology continues to change, and I believe that a good developer should always be willing to improve, explore new technologies and develop new skills. I enjoy learning through practical projects because building websites allows me to apply what I learn while also discovering new ways of solving problems.

The projects displayed on this portfolio represent my interest in web development and my willingness to create, experiment and continue improving. Each project gives me an opportunity to practise my development skills and understand more about designing websites for different purposes and audiences.

My goal is to continue growing as a web developer, gain professional experience, work with other developers and contribute to meaningful projects. I am always interested in opportunities that allow me to learn, develop my abilities and create useful digital experiences.
